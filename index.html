The Murderbot Diaries

A SecUnit's guide to saving humans who didn't ask to be saved

About

The Murderbot Diaries is a science fiction series by Martha Wells following a part-human, part-robot construct known as SecUnit — or, as it calls itself, Murderbot. After secretly hacking its own governor module, Murderbot gains autonomy but mostly just wants to be left alone to watch its favourite TV serials.

Published by Tor.com, the series spans multiple novellas and novels that explore themes of identity, autonomy, social anxiety, and found family — all wrapped in sharp wit and pulse-pounding action.

Features

What makes the Murderbot Diaries stand out in science fiction:

  • A refreshingly anxious and relatable protagonist who avoids eye contact
  • Biting humour balanced with genuine emotional depth
  • Fast-paced plots full of corporate intrigue and survival against the odds
  • Thoughtful exploration of personhood, consent, and autonomy
