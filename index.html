Checkly

The todo app that actually gets things done

About

Checkly is a beautifully simple todo list application designed to help you capture, organise, and complete tasks without getting in your way. No subscriptions, no clutter — just your list and a satisfying checkmark.

Built for individuals and small teams who are tired of bloated productivity tools, Checkly works on all your devices and syncs instantly so your tasks are always where you need them.

Features

Everything you need to stay on top of your tasks:

  • Create and organise tasks with projects, tags, and due dates
  • Real-time sync across all your devices
  • Smart reminders that nudge you at the right moment
  • Collaborate with teammates by sharing lists and assigning tasks

Recent highlights

  1. Recurring tasks — set it once, never forget it again
  2. Focus mode — hide everything except today's tasks
  3. Productivity insights — see how many tasks you complete each week
